Danmakui should have been a prototype game meant for portfolio showing but it still not finished.
A Top-down shooter, you control a fire mage stranded in the desert in between cliffs after her group was ambushed, you have to search for rests of cargo so that your companion, right now unable to walk, builds something capable of getting both of you out of there. They are surrounded by monsters brought as part of the ambush, you will have to fight or avoid them, find enough cargo and go back to the refuge where your companion awaits.
Developed on Unity, uses Star * pathfinding for the enemies, which have different behaviors like going directly to the player, wait until the player is close enough, or protect other monsters attacking only if the player attacks first.
The first enemies maintain pressure on the player with the the second become a consequence of exploration.
The terrain uses a procedural system of dungeons that visually are like the different levels of the canyon.
There are 5 different types of enemies and a boss character still not developed.

La.Per.Luz

Dungeon Master/Game Master · Worldbuilding

La.Per.Luz is a TTRPG campaign on-going since 2014, many of the games, stories, illustrations and manga by me are based on events of this world, starred by the players themselves as much as the characters from the world itself.

Currently it is reaching the end stage of the story and I making a fanzine containing illustrations of key moments my players experienced.

La.Per.Luz, occurs on Lander Rha, a post-apocalyptic fantasy world. All Huraldic life existed on Varonyt, the only city: A Megalopolis of 400 million people, from one day to the next the city was silenced, the only survivors being the tourists exploring the continent; Anyone trying to go back died after only 20 steps into the silenced zone, the city still standing over the horizon.

Helpless, the survivors discovered other huraldics, other beings and other cities: abandoned ruins, but proof of life beyond Varonyt. Slowly, they rediscovered magic and technology of the ancestors

Some animals and plants are reminiscing of those of South America while many others are completely fantastical and alien.

Some look to perfect the martial techniques of the land while others dedicate their lives to the laws of the different city-states, gods or spirits.

In between the commerce, the survival and the traveling, the elemental orb of light is broken and fragments of it start appearing all around the continent.

No matter where it appears: it reveals all the secrets being hidden, both the good and bad.

A single fragment gives the opportunity to remember that which could be salvation... or awake that which should have been forgotten.
